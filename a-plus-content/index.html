Questions sellers ask

Amazon A+ Content — Common Questions

Can you design A+ Content without access to my Seller Central account?

Yes. We can prepare copy, photography directions, comparison graphics and image modules for your review and delivery. Publishing directly into your Seller Central account requires Amazon-approved role access and your explicit permission.

Do you build Amazon seller software as well as A+ Content?

Yes. SMAT Designs develops software, APIs, ecommerce integrations and the EzSeller seller-operations product. Software authorization and Seller Central Enhanced Brand Content publishing permissions are separate.

How much does Amazon A+ Content design cost?

We quote projects based on ASIN count, number of modules, photography and copy requirements, product variants, revisions and publishing scope. Request a written proposal for specific deliverables and pricing.

Does SMAT Designs guarantee Amazon approval or sales results?

No. Amazon controls role approvals and A+ Content publication decisions. We prepare content against the relevant guidelines and verify actual publication when access permits, but we do not promise Amazon approval, ranking gains or conversion lifts.

Requirements

Draft & Review

Deliver A+ module plan, images, copy, comparison information and a seller approval checkpoint.

03 / Publication

Verify & Maintain

Seller publishes, or grants narrow role access for our team to publish; confirm Amazon's actual review outcome and published content.

Seller Central access: Content design and asset delivery can be provided without direct Seller Central permissions. When a seller requests that SMAT perform publishing/maintenance inside the seller's account, approved Enhanced Brand Content permissions must be granted by that seller after Amazon role approval. Access is specific, revocable and never inferred from an engagement.
