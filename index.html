QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked

What industries do you specialize in?

We work across consumer products, ecommerce, custom software, seller operations, fashion, and packaging. Founded in 2014, our studio combines practical operational experience with creative production and engineering.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines are scoped individually. Simple design deliverables may take weeks; software projects vary with requirements, security, third-party approvals, testing, and deployment dependencies. We agree on milestones before work begins.

Can you deliver production-ready files on first submission?

We prepare production files with the relevant manufacturer specifications and review requirements before delivery. Final acceptance depends on the manufacturer, materials and applicable rules; no acceptance guarantee is implied.

What about cannabis compliance?

We design packaging against the specifications and jurisdictional requirements provided for a project. Clients and qualified regulatory professionals remain responsible for final legal and compliance review.

Do you provide tech packs and manufacturing specs?

Technical specifications, measurements, material requirements and production instructions can be included when those deliverables are within the agreed scope.

Can you build ecommerce sites?

Yes. Depending on scope, we build storefronts, product pages, integrations, checkout workflows and operational tooling. Performance is measured after launch and business outcomes are not guaranteed.

What's the pricing structure?

Custom software, design and engineering are quoted by project after scope review. For existing packaging work, packages have historically started around $4,000 per SKU; final quotes depend on deliverables. EzSeller subscription tiers and their availability are detailed on our Software page.
