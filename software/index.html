01 / Ecommerce and marketplace operations

EzSeller

Seller operations software designed to help businesses understand what is happening across their Amazon product catalog, inventory, orders, reimbursements and operational workflow. Developed from SMAT's own multi-brand selling experience.

Catalog clarity

Listings and product identity

Organize listings, ASINs, SKUs, FNSKUs and marketplace-specific product identity. Surface conflicting or stale source records for review before actions are taken.

Reconciliation

Inventory, orders and fees

Bring together available order, fulfillment, inventory and financial evidence to inspect discrepancies rather than relying on a single dashboard summary.

Automation

Reports and agent workflows

Structure repeatable reports, investigations and tool-assisted workflows with evidence and verification steps. Integrations depend on approved data access and seller permission.

Access status: EzSeller is being developed and evaluated. Public Amazon seller authorization and SP-API connectivity are subject to Amazon approval; do not interpret this page as an offer of a currently approved Selling Partner Appstore integration. Features can change before public release.
