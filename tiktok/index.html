Official commerce-channel integration operated by SMAT Designs.

What CommerceOS does

CommerceOS is SMAT Designs' commerce operations platform. It helps authorized businesses connect supported sales channels to centralized product, order, inventory, and performance workflows.

TikTok integration

The TikTok integration uses TikTok's official OAuth authorization flow. A user is sent to TikTok to sign in and approve the permissions requested by the application. CommerceOS never asks for or stores a user's TikTok password.

How data is used

CommerceOS processes only TikTok data that the user authorizes and that TikTok has approved for this application. The data is used to provide the connected-account features requested by that user, maintain the integration, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service.

User control

Authorization is optional. Users may decline authorization or revoke access through TikTok. Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to support@smatdesigns.com.
