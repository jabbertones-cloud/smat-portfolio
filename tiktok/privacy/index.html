CommerceOS Privacy Policy

Effective: August 20, 2026

SMAT Designs operates CommerceOS. This policy explains how CommerceOS handles information when a user connects a TikTok account.

Information we process

When a user authorizes TikTok, CommerceOS may process authorization identifiers, access and refresh tokens, granted scopes, basic account information, and other data returned by TikTok APIs that the user has authorized and that TikTok has approved for the application.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate the connection, provide requested CommerceOS integration features, synchronize authorized commerce information, maintain security, troubleshoot integrations, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Credentials and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are handled server-side and are not displayed on the public website. CommerceOS does not ask users to provide their TikTok password.

Sharing and sale

SMAT Designs does not sell TikTok authorization data. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate and secure CommerceOS, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention and deletion

We retain integration information only as reasonably necessary to operate and secure the service or satisfy legal obligations. A user may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok. To request deletion of CommerceOS-held TikTok integration data, email support@smatdesigns.com. We will verify the request and delete data that we are not legally required to retain.

Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: support@smatdesigns.com.
