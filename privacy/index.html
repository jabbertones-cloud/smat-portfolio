To respond to inquiries, provide agreed services or support, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, deliver requested software functions, manage billing and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or Amazon Selling Partner data.

Information may be processed by providers acting on our behalf, including website hosting, email, payment processing, hosting infrastructure and security services, when needed to provide a requested service. We use HTTPS for our published website and apply access controls and operational security practices appropriate to the service. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Do not email passwords, API tokens, security codes or sensitive credentials.

We retain inquiry and service information only for legitimate business, operational and legal needs, and delete or deidentify information when no longer needed, subject to lawful recordkeeping duties. Software account and marketplace-data retention may differ by contract or product-specific notice. You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information by emailing the address above. We will verify requests as appropriate and explain any retention obligations.

Updates

We may update this notice when services or law change. The effective date above reflects the latest published revision. For specific product policies or access requests, contact info@smatdesigns.com.
